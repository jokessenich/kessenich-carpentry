Kessenich Carpentry is a residential remodeling and carpentry contractor based in Ann Arbor, Michigan, owned by John Kessenich and licensed as a Michigan residential builder (license #242601393). We remodel bathrooms, kitchens and basements, remove load-bearing walls and build custom carpentry for homes in Washtenaw County and western Wayne County. Kessenich Home Service, our repair team, handles smaller jobs.

Questions

Good to know

Is the walkthrough really free?

Yes. We come to your home, measure the space and talk through what you want. No cost and no obligation.

How does pricing work?

Remodels get a fixed-price proposal for the agreed scope, with allowances for finishes you haven't chosen yet. Repairs and small jobs are billed hourly.

Do you pull permits?

Yes. When a project needs a permit, we apply for it and schedule the inspections.

What areas do you serve?

Ann Arbor, Ypsilanti, Dexter, Saline, Milan, Plymouth, Northville and surrounding communities in Washtenaw and western Wayne County.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. Kessenich Home Service handles repairs, installs and other small projects around the house.
